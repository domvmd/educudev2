# Product Mission

> Last Updated: 2025-08-16
> Version: 1.0.0

## Pitch

Educude is an AI-powered educational platform that helps students and educators transform PDF textbooks, lectures, and notes into intelligent quizzes and comprehensive study materials by providing questions targeting higher levels of Bloom's Taxonomy to promote deep learning and critical thinking.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- **Self-Directed Learners**: College/graduate students and professional learners seeking deep understanding of subject matter and exam preparation
- **Educators**: Teachers, professors, and tutors who need to create challenging assessments efficiently

### User Personas

**Self-Directed Learner** (18-35 years old)
- **Role:** College/Graduate Student or Professional Learner
- **Context:** Studying complex subjects requiring conceptual understanding beyond memorization
- **Pain Points:** Lack of practice with application and analysis questions, traditional study methods focus on rote memorization
- **Goals:** Deep understanding of subject matter, excel in exams that test critical thinking

**Educator** (25-65 years old)
- **Role:** Teacher, Professor, or Tutor
- **Context:** Teaching courses requiring assessment of higher-order thinking skills
- **Pain Points:** Time constraints in creating higher-order questions, requires significant pedagogical expertise
- **Goals:** Create challenging assessments efficiently, improve student learning outcomes

## The Problem

### Time-Consuming Question Creation

Creating pedagogically sound questions at higher Bloom's Taxonomy levels (Analyze, Evaluate, Create) requires significant time and expertise. Manual creation of a single high-quality question can take 15-30 minutes.

**Our Solution:** AI-powered generation creates sophisticated questions in under 2 seconds per question.

### Focus on Lower-Order Thinking

Current study methods and available tools primarily focus on memorization and basic comprehension, failing to prepare students for exams and real-world applications requiring critical thinking.

**Our Solution:** Specifically targets Bloom's levels 4-6 with multi-concept questions that promote deep learning.

### Fragmented Study Materials

Students often juggle PDFs, lecture recordings, presentation slides, and handwritten notes without a unified system to synthesize information across sources.

**Our Solution:** Unified platform that processes and connects all content types for comprehensive study material generation.

## Differentiators

### Multi-Source Intelligence

Unlike traditional quiz generators that process single documents, we synthesize information from textbooks, audio lectures, slides, and personal notes simultaneously. This results in questions that test understanding across different representations of the same concepts.

### Bloom's Taxonomy Alignment

Unlike competitors focusing on basic recall questions, we specifically generate questions at higher cognitive levels (Analyze, Evaluate, Create). This results in 85% of questions correctly aligned with targeted Bloom's levels versus typical 20-30% for standard generators.

### Dual-Format Processing

Unlike simple text extraction tools, we use Marker's dual Markdown + JSON output for both human-readable study materials and precise AI processing. This results in 96%+ accuracy on complex academic layouts with preserved formulas, tables, and figures.

## Key Features

### Core Features

- **Multi-Format Content Import:** Process PDFs, audio lectures, presentation slides, and handwritten notes with high accuracy
- **Intelligent Quiz Generation:** Create questions targeting specific Bloom's Taxonomy levels with multi-concept synthesis
- **Semantic Search & Retrieval:** Find and connect related concepts across all imported materials using vector embeddings
- **Comprehensive Answer Keys:** Generate detailed explanations, rubrics, and source references for every question

### Collaboration Features

- **Note-Taking & Annotation:** Interactive markdown editor with highlighting, margin notes, and concept tagging
- **Smart Study Guides:** Auto-generate summaries and study materials from annotated content
- **Progress Tracking:** Monitor learning progress with detailed analytics and spaced repetition
- **Export Flexibility:** Export to PDF, Anki, Quizlet, and other popular study formats
